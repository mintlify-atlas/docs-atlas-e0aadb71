## What Cookie Monster does

Cookie Monster adds a layer of calculated insight on top of the standard Cookie Clicker UI. At the heart of it is the **Payback Period (PP)** — a single number that tells you how long it will take to recoup the cost of any building or upgrade. Lower is better.

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Cookie Clicker">
    Navigate to [orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker](https://orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker/) and let the game fully load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load Cookie Monster">
    Paste the bookmarklet into your browser's address bar, or run the one-liner in the browser console:
    ```javascript
    Game.LoadMod('https://cookiemonsterteam.github.io/CookieMonster/dist/CookieMonster.js');
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the notification">
    Cookie Monster shows a popup confirming the version that loaded. The shop immediately gains color-coded borders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the settings">
    Open the Cookie Clicker **Options** menu. Scroll down to find the **Cookie Monster** section where every feature can be toggled on or off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Cookie Monster is not a cheat tool. It surfaces information and calculations derived from the game's own data. Every helper feature — from golden cookie alerts to PP color-coding — can be turned off individually.
</Note>
