Cookie Monster adds its own settings panel directly inside Cookie Clicker's native Options menu. Open the game's Options menu (the button in the top-right of the screen), then scroll down past the standard game options to find the **Cookie Monster** section. Every toggle, colour picker, and numeric input described throughout this documentation lives there.

## Finding the Settings Panel

When you open Options, the Cookie Monster settings block appears below the base game's options. Settings are grouped under collapsible category headers — click the **+** or **−** next to a header to expand or collapse that group. Your collapsed/expanded state is saved automatically alongside the rest of your Cookie Monster preferences.

## The Favourites Section

One of the most useful quality-of-life features in Cookie Monster's settings UI is the **Favourites** system, controlled by the `FavouriteSettings` toggle.

<Tip>
  Pin the settings you tweak most often — such as `CPSMode`, `Scale`, or `PPRigidelMode` — to the Favourites section so you can reach them instantly without scrolling through all nine categories.
</Tip>

The `FavouriteSettings` setting has three modes:

| Mode | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **OFF** | No stars are shown; no Favourites section appears. |
| **ON** | A ⭐ star appears before every setting. Click a star to pin that setting to a **Favourites** section that appears at the very top of the Cookie Monster settings block. |
| **Locked** | Stars are hidden, but the Favourites section continues to display any settings you previously pinned. Use this once you have finished curating your favourites and no longer want the stars cluttering the UI. |

## The Eight Setting Categories

Cookie Monster organises all its options into nine groups. The table below summarises what each one governs.

<Accordion title="1 · Calculation">
  Controls how Cookie Monster computes cookie-per-second figures and payback times throughout the UI.

  - **CPSMode** — switch between *current* CPS and a rolling *average* CPS for all time calculations.
  - **AvgCPSHist** — set the rolling-average window (10 s up to 30 min).
  - **AvgClicksHist** — set the rolling-average window for click-rate calculations (1 s up to 30 s).
  - **CalcWrink** — include wrinkler cookie absorption in CPS and timing estimates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2 · Notation">
  Controls how Cookie Monster formats large numbers and time durations everywhere it renders its own text.

  - **Scale** — choose from Game's default, Metric, Short scale, Short scale abbreviated, Scientific notation, or Engineering notation.
  - **ScaleDecimals** — 1, 2, or 3 decimal places.
  - **ScaleSeparator** — swap the decimal and thousands separators (`.` vs `,`).
  - **ScaleCutoff** — the numeric threshold at which Cookie Monster begins applying the chosen scale (default 999,999).
  - **TimeFormat** — `XXd XXh XXm XXs`, `XX:XX:XX:XX:XX` (digital clock), or `XXx XXx` (two most significant units only).
  - **DetailedTime** — toggle verbose time strings in stats and tooltips.
  - **PPDisplayTime** — show Payback Period as a raw calculated value or as an approximate time unit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="3 · Colours">
  Controls colour-coding of buildings and upgrades, PP-based filters, and all nine customisable colour swatches.

  - **BuildColour** — enable or disable colour-coding on buildings.
  - **PPOnlyConsiderBuyable** — mark items you cannot currently afford as if they are the worst option.
  - **PPExcludeTop** — exclude the 1st, 2nd, or 3rd best options from colour-coding.
  - **PPRigidelMode** — ignore all buy-1 options to keep your total building count ending in 10 for the Rigidel pantheon god.
  - **PPSecondsLowerLimit** — treat any item costing fewer than X seconds of CPS as optimal regardless of its PP rank.
  - **ColourBlue / Green / Yellow / Orange / Red / Purple / Gray / Pink / Brown** — the nine customisable colour swatches used throughout the UI.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="4 · BarsDisplay">
  Controls the bottom building-info bar, the timer bar, the upgrade colour bar, sorting, the golden cookie countdown timer, the favicon, and wrinkler buttons.

  - **BotBar** — toggle the bottom bar that shows building CPS and cost information.
  - **TimerBar** — toggle the bar showing golden cookie, season popup, and frenzy timers.
  - **TimerBarPos** — place the timer bar at the top-left or bottom of the screen.
  - **TimerBarOverlay** — overlay seconds and/or percentage remaining on each timer segment.
  - **AutosaveTimerBar** — add an autosave countdown segment to the timer bar.
  - **UpBarColour / UpgradeBarFixedPos** — upgrade colour bar and its scroll-lock behaviour.
  - **SortBuildings** — default order, PP of ×1 purchase, PP of selected bulk mode, or price-to-next-achievement.
  - **SortUpgrades** — default order or by PP.
  - **UpgradesNeverCollapse** — keep upgrade sections always fully expanded.
  - **DragonAuraInfo** — show CPS and cost deltas in the dragon aura selection interface.
  - **GrimoireBar** — overlay a magic-meter refill timer on the Grimoire widget.
  - **GCTimer** — display a countdown on the golden cookie while it is visible.
  - **Favicon** — update the browser tab favicon when a golden or wrath cookie is present.
  - **WrinklerButtons** — add pop-all and pop-fattest buttons below the big cookie.
  - **HideSectionsButtons** — add hide/show toggle buttons for the buildings and upgrades panels.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="5 · Tooltip">
  Controls extra information injected into building, upgrade, grimoire, wrinkler, stock market, garden, and pantheon tooltips, as well as bank-safety warnings.

  - **TooltipBuildUpgrade** — extra PP and timing data in building/upgrade tooltips.
  - **TooltipAmor** — amortization breakdown in building tooltips.
  - **ToolWarnLucky / ToolWarnLuckyFrenzy** — warn when a purchase would drop your bank below the threshold for maximum Lucky! rewards.
  - **ToolWarnConjure / ToolWarnConjureFrenzy** — warn for Conjure Baked Goods thresholds.
  - **ToolWarnEdifice** — warn for the Spontaneous Edifice spell threshold.
  - **ToolWarnUser** — custom warning at a user-defined multiple of CPS.
  - **ToolWarnBon / ToolWarnPos** — configure whether warnings account for the CPS bonus from buying, and where warning boxes appear.
  - **TooltipGrim** — extra Grimoire spell information in tooltips.
  - **TooltipWrink** — show the cookie yield of popping a specific wrinkler.
  - **TooltipLump** — show the current sugar lump type in the lump tooltip.
  - **TooltipPlots** — show a tooltip for garden plants that yield a cookie reward.
  - **TooltipStocks** — extra information in Stock Market tooltips.
  - **TooltipPantheon** — extra information in Pantheon god tooltips.
  - **TooltipAscendButton** — extra information in the Ascend button tooltip.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="6 · Statistics">
  Controls the Cookie Monster statistics extension inside Cookie Clicker's Stats menu.

  - **Stats** — master toggle for the entire Cookie Monster stats section.
  - **MissingUpgrades** — list upgrades you have not yet unlocked or purchased.
  - **MissingAchievements** — list normal achievements you have not yet earned.
  - **UpStats** — update statistics every 1 second instead of the default 5 seconds.
  - **HeavenlyChipsTarget** — set a prestige target; Cookie Monster will count progress towards it in the prestige section.
  - **ShowMissedGC** — display a counter of golden cookies you have let expire without clicking.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="7 · NotificationGeneral">
  Controls the two settings that apply across all event notifications.

  - **Title** — update the browser tab title with golden-cookie and season-popup timers; a third mode highlights the tab title only when a cookie or reindeer actually spawns.
  - **GeneralSound** — when OFF, Cookie Monster notification sounds play regardless of the base game's volume slider, so you can mute in-game music while still hearing alerts.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="8 · Notification sub-categories (GC, FC, Season, Garden, Magic, Wrinkler, MaxWrinkler)">
  Each event type has its own sub-group with identical controls: a browser **Notification** toggle, a **Flash** toggle, a **Flash colour** swatch, a **Sound** toggle, a **Volume** slider (0–100), and a **Sound URL** field for a custom audio clip.

  | Sub-category | Trigger event |
  |---|---|
  | **NotificationGC** | Golden or wrath cookie spawns |
  | **NotificationFC** | Fortune cookie appears on the ticker |
  | **NotificationSea** | Season popup (reindeer, Easter egg, etc.) spawns |
  | **NotificationGard** | Garden ticks |
  | **NotificationMagi** | Grimoire magic meter reaches maximum |
  | **NotificationWrink** | A wrinkler appears |
  | **NotificationWrinkMax** | The maximum number of wrinklers is reached |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="9 · Miscellaneous">
  Holds two settings that do not belong to any specific feature group: bulk-buy protection and the Favourites UI toggle.

  - **BulkBuyBlock** — when ON, prevents partial bulk purchases; the buy button does nothing if you cannot afford the full batch (default ON).
  - **FavouriteSettings** — show, hide, or lock the star icons that let you pin any setting to the Favourites section at the top of the Cookie Monster settings block.
</Accordion>
